Overview

An incubatee needed a business card that felt investor-ready: clean typography, a confident mark, and a layout that works as well in a networking hall as it does as a PDF attach.

crisis_alert

The Challenge

Early-stage teams often ship generic templates. The card had to signal credibility without looking over-designed, and it had to reproduce cleanly in both digital and offset print.

lightbulb

The Solution

Designed a restrained geometric identity, hierarchy for name/role/contact, and a two-sided layout with enough whitespace to feel premium in the hand.
